"Memories weigh upon the soul. Endure them, for they are the only path to truth."

A mysterious woman who refers to herself as a gravekeeper. I first encountered her three years ago in the Cognito Sea. So much remains unknown: her true identity, how she really makes a living, even where she lives. However, I can at least be certain that she harbours no ill intentions. It seems she's endeavouring to keep something hidden, but... what could it be?
