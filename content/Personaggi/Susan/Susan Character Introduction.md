A courier at the Sparrow Post, forever dashing about to deliver letters for others, yet seldom speaking of herself. Conveying one's true meaning is a challenge for most people, and especially so for Susan. Long stretches of silence have dulled her ability to express herself. She is now working hard to recover, but still relies on her automavian for assistance. Do be patient with her.
